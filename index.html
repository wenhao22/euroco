About Euroco

Euroco creates soft, colourful pipe cleaner flowers by hand. Each bloom is carefully shaped into a lasting arrangement that can be kept as room decor, given as a gift, or made into a custom bouquet.

Unlike fresh flowers, our handmade blooms do not wilt. Choose your colours, flower style, and bouquet size for something personal and long-lasting.
